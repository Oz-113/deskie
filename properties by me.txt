This project has come so far,

 it could track a virtual volume and adjust the slider accordingly. After tinkering around I have found out that not tracking any volume was a better approach, now just a volume up or volume down icon appears on the screen when triggered, in this project there is a matrix rotary encoder encoder that utilizes interrupts to determine the direction and so it delivers almost perfect direction and step values. 

LittleFS is used to utilise the 16MB Flash for binary raw files of the frames, the frames are then read to a frame buffer which allows the TFT_eSPI library to push the Image to the allocated Sprite which gets rid of flickering and allows updating the screen without clearing behind what was present. 

Preferences library allows for saving and reading of values which was a useless feature after I abandoned the virtual volume and adjustable speed idea, could still be used to remotely wake up the computer with a web server or another things.

After discarding the idea of the Arc following the virtual volume too, it is just a gif player without any ui elements, pause next and previous could maybe add something but new ideas must be found to further elevate the feeling of a control center rather than just a fancy gif player.
